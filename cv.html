Executive Summary

Computational proteomics scientist with more than 20 years of interdisciplinary experience spanning computational biology, bioinformatics, scientific software engineering, proteomics, mass spectrometry, and pharmaceutical drug discovery. Experienced in designing and deploying AI-native computational platforms that integrate machine learning, probabilistic modeling, graph algorithms, large language models, foundation models, and classical bioinformatics to automate complex scientific workflows. Chief Machine Learning Scientist at ProteoBio, where the company's AI strategy is architected and the ProteoAgent Server multi-agent platform is developed for automated proteomics and proteoform analysis. Concurrently AI/ML Lead Scientist at the Northwestern University Proteomics Center of Excellence, leading development and evaluation of machine learning methods for top-down proteomics. Earlier experience includes six years of computational proteomics, bioinformatics, and AI-enabled drug discovery across ActivX Biosciences, Vertex Pharmaceuticals, and Exelixis.
